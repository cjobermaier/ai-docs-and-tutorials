The tutorials on this site will help you improve your existing documentation or tutorials.  You will learn how to imropve your content using specific workflows that run AI to focus on specific tasks. 

You will not learn how to generate content from scratch or have AI write whole content sections.

## Who this is for

- Developers who write documentation for their projects
- Technical writers writing complex content
- Educators who create tutorials

## Requirements

I wrote the tutorials for Claude Code, but also tested them with Copilot. However, you can use the fundementals and resources with most AI coding assistants.

## My AI-assisted philosophy

Using AI doesn't mean using AI to write all your content from scratch. These tutorials keep YOU in control and treats the AI as a tool to use and not an omnipotent solution. AI slop sucks. Lets fix that.
